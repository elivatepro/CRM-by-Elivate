# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CRM by Elivate is used by the internal Elivate team. Users need a focused workspace for managing customer relationships, people, companies, opportunities, tasks, and related operational records.

## Product Purpose

Provide Elivate with a self-hosted CRM that presents as an Elivate product while retaining Twenty's proven CRM workflows. Success means team members can enter the application, recognize the Elivate identity immediately, and complete CRM work without vendor-brand confusion or unnecessary friction.

## Brand Personality

Professional, confident, and minimal. The product should feel deliberate and trustworthy, with the Elivate identity present at key recognition points without overpowering the work interface.

## Anti-references

- Default Twenty branding on user-facing entry screens
- Flashy, decorative, or over-animated authentication experiences
- Generic SaaS styling that could belong to any company
- Artificial-looking gradients, effects, or altered versions of the Elivate mark
- Visual changes that reinvent familiar product controls

## Design Principles

1. Establish Elivate identity immediately at entry points.
2. Preserve familiar CRM interactions and change only what improves recognition or clarity.
3. Keep branding restrained so customer data and daily work remain primary.
4. Preserve the approved Elivate mark exactly rather than embellishing it.
5. Verify changes on the real deployed surface before treating them as complete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient text and control contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
